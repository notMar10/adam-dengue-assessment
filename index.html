Dengue awareness

Important facts to protect yourself and your community

What is dengue?

Dengue is a viral infection spread mainly by Aedes mosquitoes (often active during the day). It is common in tropical and subtropical areas and can affect anyone, including children and adults.

Common signs & symptoms
  • Sudden high fever (often 3–7 days)
  • Severe headache and pain behind the eyes
  • Joint and muscle pain
  • Skin rash, nausea, or vomiting
  • Mild bleeding (nose or gums) in some cases
Seek care immediately if you notice
  • Severe abdominal pain or persistent vomiting
  • Bleeding gums, nosebleeds, or blood in vomit or stool
  • Drowsiness, restlessness, or difficulty breathing
  • Cold, clammy skin or very low urine output

These may be signs of severe dengue — go to a hospital or clinic right away.

Prevention tips
  • 🪣 Remove stagnant water where mosquitoes breed (containers, tires, gutters)
  • 🦟 Use repellent, long sleeves, and window screens
  • 🏠 Keep surroundings clean and cover water storage
  • 🏥 Rest, drink fluids, and consult a doctor if fever persists

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about dengue and this tool

Is A.D.A.M. a medical diagnosis?

No. This tool provides a screening score based on symptoms you select. Only a healthcare professional can diagnose dengue.

What does the score percentage mean?

It reflects how many weighted symptoms you reported. A higher score suggests higher concern — not a confirmed infection.

Should I go to the hospital?

Go immediately if you have severe pain, persistent vomiting, bleeding, difficulty breathing, or feel very weak or confused.

Is my data stored?

No. Your selections are not saved on a server. Results are passed only in the page URL when you view your score.
